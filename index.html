Step-by-Step Usage Guide

  1. Upload Egrantz File: Upload the Excel (.xlsx / .xls) or CSV file downloaded from the Egrantz site. The first row (reference number with the sanctioned amount in brackets) is read automatically.
  2. Compare with Master Sheet: Upload the master sheet (Adm. No., Student Name, Course, Academic Year). It is remembered in this browser. Students are matched by Admission Number, or by name, course and batch when the Admission Number is blank. Review rows marked in yellow/red and correct the course names if needed.
  3. Configure Special Fees: Fee rates are kept per Academic Year (taken from the Period column, e.g. July 2024 → 2024-2025) for UG I–IV and PG I–II. Enter 0 where a fee is not applicable. Lab fee is calculated as Special Fee minus the other fees.
  4. Disbursement: For each student tick where the money goes – Refund to Egrantz, Full amount to student (except ABLC), individual University Special Fee heads / Exam Fee to student (unticked heads go to University), or partly paid Tuition Fee.
  5. Download: Download the Formatted Excel with Account Summary and the Student Refund List.

Step 2: Compare with Master Sheet

Course Name Mapping

Course names in the Egrantz file are replaced with the Actual Course Name in all outputs. It is filled automatically by comparing the degree and subject words with the courses in the master sheet (e.g. BA HONOURS IN ENGLISH 4 YEAR → BA English, BCOM COOP → BCom). Type a name to set it yourself (remembered in this browser); clear the box to go back to the automatic name. UG/PG decides which fee rates apply.

Step 4: Disbursement

Year-wise Defaults

Choosing a default ticks / unticks the boxes below for all students of that year. You can then change individual students.

Per-Student Disbursement

Refund to Egrantz: whole amount of the student is returned to Egrantz Dept. Enter the head of account to which it is remitted.
Full → Student: whole amount except ABLC is refunded to the student (ABLC still goes to Hostel).
Univ. Special Fees / Exam Fee → Student: ticked fees are refunded to the student, unticked fees go to University.
Partial Tuition: enter the amount the student already paid; it is refunded to the student and the balance goes to 0202-01-103-99.

Heads of Account

Text shown in the Head of Account column of the Account Summary, e.g. the account number or "Kannur University - A/c No. ...". Saved in this browser; leave blank to use the default.
